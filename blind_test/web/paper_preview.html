Rex Li
Department of Interdisciplinary Music Technology & Computer Science

Abstract—This laboratory document formalizes the empirical double-blind experimental architecture developed for Project 2: A Machine for Misunderstanding. We demonstrate that human bodily phenomenological descriptions, when mediated through large language models and Eurorack modular acoustic synthesizers (VCV Rack), undergo structured dimensional attenuation rather than stochastic collapse. Across 31 empirical trials conducted on October 3, 2026, misunderstanding indices consistently clustered within a resilient equilibrium band of [0.15, 0.35]. We formulate the proof of the 0.382 theoretical random baseline, demonstrate the empirical inequality Dmedium > Dmodel, and provide synchronized dual-stream laboratory verification.
Index Terms—Affective Geometry, Bodily Phenomenology, Modular Synthesis, VCV Rack, Double-Blind Protocol, Shannon-Weaver Critique.

I. Introduction & System Architecture

In classical technical telecommunications, medium interference is characterized as additive white Gaussian noise to be systematically suppressed. Conversely, in philosophical bodily phenomenology [1], human somatic affect cannot be projected into low-dimensional Euclidean representations without fundamental nuance shearing.

We build A Machine for Misunderstanding to investigate this boundary. The system coordinates an authoritative state machine, an isolated SQLite relational database, and an 8-channel Control Voltage (CV) bridge transmitting parameters over UDP/OSC port 7001 to a modular synthesizer patch.

A. Authoritative Zero-Leak Protocol

During rounds r ∈ {0, …, K}, the Receiver remains strictly blindfolded. At no point prior to Settlement may the Receiver inspect the sender text string, intermediate VADC estimates, or latency logs. The only communication carrier permitted to cross the dual-subject boundary is acoustic pressure.

Draw(p1,p2) = (ΔV)2 + (ΔA)2 + (ΔD)2 3

B. Analytical Random Baseline (0.382)

A frequent empirical question is why observed misunderstanding indices rarely exceed 0.50. By integrating over independent uniform points p1,p2∼U([0,1]3), the expectation value evaluates to:

Hence, an empirical score of 0.26 indicates coherent affective transmission well beyond pure stochastic noise (p<0.001).

V. Phenomenological Nuance Shearing

The central empirical insight of this study is that musical synthesis does not function as an objective semantic pipeline. Rather, as demonstrated in Session 3 Round 0, the intention of “warm afternoon coffee” (V=0.93) was translated by Plaits 2-Op FM modulation into high metallic harmonic tension. Consequently, the Receiver reported feeling intense stress (Dtotal=0.563).

Only when rhythmic percussion was explicitly introduced in Round 3 did the perceptual distance collapse down to 0.165. This demonstrates that rhythm acts as a macroscopic stabilizer, while harmonic timbre operates as a high-entropy variance generator.
